# Frozen evaluation set v1 and ADTOF baseline — 2026-10-16

A fixed set of 139 recordings with documented training overlap and fine instrument
labels, plus the current ADTOF engine's baseline on it. Every future BeatSight model is
judged on this set, and nothing in it may be used for training, validation or tuning
(`exclusions.json`).

before building; `manifest.json` with input/reference SHA-256 per item; `exclusions.json`;

All 139 runs succeeded. MDB and ENST numbers reproduce earlier measurements.

**Tier A vs B is confounded, not proof of overlap.** Drummer 1's annotated kicks are weak in
the audio. The kick-mic attack median is 10.8 dB (vs 40.9/28.8 dB for drummers 2/3), 72.5%
show a ≥6 dB attack (vs 99%+), and the wet-mix low-band rise is 9.4 dB (vs about 21 dB). Even
clearly attacked drummer 1 kicks are found only 45% of the time (vs 97–98%). Quiet mixing
explains part of the gap. Kit generalization and possible training on drummers 2–3 remain
possible explanations that this evidence cannot separate. Quiet, feathered kicks are a real
hard case, so they stay in the set.

**Resolution gap.** Share of each fine class caught by the right family (all tiers): kick .81,
snare .60, closed hi-hat .79, pedal hi-hat .16, open hi-hat .66, high/mid/low tom .40/.36/.57,
ride .67, ride bell .82, crash .54, China .42, splash .14. Side-stick, cowbell and other are
never output. Beyond these misses, ADTOF cannot say which hi-hat, tom or cymbal it heard.

## Derived exclusions

`exclusions-derived-musdb.json` (added after the baseline; hash in `evidence/protocol.sha256`)
makes the "any other rendering" rule explicit for MUSDB18-HQ on D:: all 31 STAR test songs are
MUSDB songs, and MUSDB also contains MDB's 80s Rock, Beatles and Punk. Those 31 MUSDB folders
must never be used for training, validation, tuning or pseudo-labelling.
